Inspired by the desire to preserve the distinct character of the hand crafted signage and posters that emerged in the early twentieth century of the in the homonymous neighbourhood in Buenos Aires. It evokes the sense of care and community that brought life to this community in this South American urban landscape.

The combination a large x-height, short descenders and wide apertures makes this typeface highly legible. While some of the details show off it’s distinct character. For example; the capital Q has a quirky little tail and the capital J has a left-hanging crossbar. All of this has lead to the typeface being found on over 16 million websites and averages approximately 9.82 Billion API requests per week.
